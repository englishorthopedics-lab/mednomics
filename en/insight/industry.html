    Korean healthcare industry map

    From drugmakers to payers: nine segments, how each makes money, what to watch and representative listed companies.

    Traditional pharma

    How it makes money
    Sells generics, incrementally modified drugs and in-licensed products through prescriptions. Price cuts and rebate rules decide margins.
    What to watch
    Price-cut notices, outpatient prescription sales, royalty income

    Biotech platforms and licensing

    How it makes money
    Licenses drug candidates or platform technology to global pharma for upfront, milestone and royalty payments. A single trial readout can move the valuation.
    What to watch
    Deal size and returns, trial phase transitions, JPM conference news

    CDMO and biosimilars

    How it makes money
    Manufactures biologics for other companies (CDMO) or sells copies of off-patent biologics. A large share of Korea's drug exports.
    What to watch
    Order backlog, plant utilization, US tariff and drug-pricing policy
    Representative listed companies
    Samsung Biologics207940Celltrion068270

    Obesity and metabolic drugs

    How it makes money
    GLP-1 drugs are expanding the market. Korean firms target niches with their own molecules, long-acting formulations and oral versions.
    What to watch
    Local launches and reimbursement, prescription volume, oral-formulation trials
    Representative listed companies

    Aesthetic medicine

    How it makes money
    Sells consumables such as toxins, fillers and skin boosters, and laser and HIFU devices. Fees are uninsured and set by clinics; foreign patients drive demand.
    What to watch
    Foreign patient numbers, export diversification, VAT refunds on cosmetic procedures
    Representative listed companies

    Medical devices, dental and imaging

    How it makes money
    Sells equipment and materials hospitals buy: implants, dental imaging, X-ray detectors, body-composition analyzers. Overseas sales are a large share.
    What to watch
    Export results, emerging-market sales, private-equity buyouts
    Representative listed companies

    Medical AI and digital health

    How it makes money
    Sells software for image reading, clinical documentation and vital-sign analysis to hospitals and screening centers. Reimbursement decides revenue.
    What to watch
    Innovative device designation, reimbursement decisions, overseas sales
    Representative listed companies
    Lunit328130VUNO338220

    Hospital purchasing and distribution

    How it makes money
    Supplies drugs and consumables to hospitals for a margin. Many intermediaries and wholesalers are unlisted, so news and rulings are the main sources.
    What to watch
    Rules on purchasing intermediaries, private-equity deals, rebate rulings

    Payers: national insurance and private top-ups

    How it makes money
    National insurance pays most medical bills; patients and private indemnity insurance pay for uninsured care. Premiums, fees and coverage set the ceiling for the whole industry.
    What to watch
    Insurance fund balance, fee negotiations, coverage expansion, controls on uninsured care
