    Korean healthcare in numbers

    Premiums, fees, medical school quotas and exports: the numbers that set the rules of Korean healthcare, plus the dates ahead.

    Home / Insight

    Numbers · as of Nov 24, 2026

    Key numbers

    2027 health insurance premium rate 7.19%

    Frozen at the 2026 level. Approved by the Health Insurance Policy Review Committee on Sep 8, 2026

    Source · Kormedi
    Average 2027 fee increase 1.65%

    Clinics: talks failed, the review committee set 1.6%; conversion factor KRW 95.6 → 96.5

    Source · Medigate News
    2026 health insurance balance (projected) -2.84tn won

    KRW 364.4bn deficit at end-July; KRW 2.84tn deficit projected for year-end

    Source · Nocut News
    2027 medical school quota 3,548

    490 more than the 2024 quota of 3,058; all additional seats go to the regional doctor track

    Source · Financial News
    Foreign patients, 2025 2.01million

    Above 2 million for the first time since records began; dermatology accounted for 62.9%

    Source · Wikitree, citing the health ministry
    Drug exports, 2025 10.4USD bn

    First time above USD 10bn; total bio-health exports hit a record USD 27.9bn

    Source · Medigate News
    Co-pay for rare and severe intractable diseases 10→7%

    About 1.36 million patients from Dec 1; a further cut to 5% in the first half of 2028

    Source · Korea.kr
    Recovery rate from illegally opened clinics 8.8%

    1,805 sites in 2009–2025; KRW 256.3bn collected of KRW 2.92tn ordered

    Source · PPSS

    Figures reflect reports and announcements as of the date shown. Check the source for later changes.
