    Editorial policy

    Editorial policy

    How we pick, summarize and correct the daily healthcare news.

    Home / News / Editorial policy
    01

    What we pick
    Stories on health policy, hospitals and clinics, pharma and biotech, devices and AI, and research that affect clinical practice or hospital business. We aim for 5–10 a day.

    02

    Where it comes from
    Korean healthcare trade press such as Medigate News, Docdocdoc, Bosa, Dailypharm and Khanews, plus government and society announcements. When several outlets cover a story, we list them together.

    03

    How we summarize
    Dr. Jordan reads the original and writes a new headline and summary. We do not copy article text or photos; any short quotation carries quotation marks and a credit.

    04

    How we credit
    Every item lists the publisher and links to the original, where readers can find full detail and context. Most originals are in Korean.

    05

    Fact and opinion
    Summaries report only what was published. Interpretation appears only in boxes marked 'Dr. Jordan' and in 'Today in one line'.

    06

    Corrections
    If a summary differs from the source or facts change, we fix it as soon as we confirm it and note the date and change on the page.

    07

    Interests
    The author is a practicing physician. Any advertising or sponsorship will be labeled next to the item concerned.

    08

    Copyright
    Copyright in cited articles belongs to their publishers. We will remove a link or summary if a publisher asks.

    Contact
    Send tips, corrections or takedown requests via a comment or message on our Naver blog.
