    About

    I have watched up close where drugs and devices are actually used, and how hospitals buy supplies and hire people.

    I wrote about the economy and companies on my blog for years before realizing I had left out the field I know best. Mednomics fills that gap. The name joins medicine and economics.

    I write under a pen name. I want the work judged by its content and evidence, not by a name.

    Principles

    Writing principles

    1. 01
      Public sources only

      I rely on material anyone can check: news, filings, papers and policy documents. I do not use undisclosed information heard from others.

    2. 02
      No stock picks

      I explain how the industry works. I never tell you to buy or sell a particular stock.

    3. 03
      Interests disclosed

      If I ever have a financial relationship with a company I cover, I say so clearly in the article.

    4. 04
      Patients come first

      I do not write about cases that could identify a patient. Stories from practice are generalized.

    5. 05
      Sources cited

      Numbers and claims come with evidence. Errors are corrected as soon as confirmed, and the correction is noted.

    Corrections

    Corrections

    If you spot an error, please let me know. I fix the text once confirmed and update the revision date at the bottom of the article. Figures can differ between reports; when they do, I use the latest source and say so.
