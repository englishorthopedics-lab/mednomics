    Glossary

    Look up the terms used in our articles. The list grows with every new post.

    GPO
    Group Purchasing Organization. A purchasing agent that pools buying for many hospitals, negotiates with manufacturers and earns fees. Common in the US.Appears in: EP.01
    WOMAC
    A questionnaire score for pain, stiffness and function in knee and hip osteoarthritis. Studies often convert it to a 0–100 scale.Appears in: EP.04
    Purchasing intermediary (gannapsa)
    An indirect supplier that delivers medical supplies or drugs to hospitals on behalf of manufacturers and distributors. Often partly owned by the hospital foundation or the owner's relatives; its cut is sometimes called a 'toll'.Appears in: EP.01
    Tender offer
    Buying shares from many shareholders by publicly offering to purchase them outside the market.Appears in: EP.03
    Managed coverage
    A coverage type within national insurance that controls price and use but sets a high co-pay. Raised in the parliamentary audit as a route for obesity drugs.Appears in: EP.04
    Reimbursement listing
    When a drug is added to the national insurance list and can be prescribed under insurance. A company applies, then goes through assessment and price negotiation.Appears in: EP.04
    Licensing and milestones
    A deal that transfers rights to a drug to another company in return for an upfront payment and staged milestone payments. Not all of the announced value is guaranteed income.Appears in: EP.02
    Dual-punishment rebate rule
    A rule that punishes both those who offer benefits in return for drug adoption or prescriptions and the doctors or pharmacists who accept them. Introduced in 2010.Appears in: EP.06
    Payout ratio
    The share of a year's net profit paid out as dividends. Paying KRW 50 on KRW 100 of profit is a 50% payout ratio.Appears in: EP.06
    Bolt-on
    An acquisition strategy of adding related businesses to a company you already own, such as buying an intraoral scanner maker for an implant company.Appears in: EP.03
    Unfaithful disclosure designation
    A listed company flagged by the Korea Exchange for failing disclosure duties. Accumulated penalty points can lead to a trading halt or a delisting review.Appears in: EP.02
    Uninsured care (bigeubyeo)
    Care or drugs that national insurance does not cover, so the patient pays in full.Appears in: EP.04 · EP.05
    Notice of income reclassification
    A tax office notice telling a company that a payment is a certain person's income of a certain type and that tax must be withheld. The company then has to withhold accordingly.Appears in: EP.06
    Substance-over-form principle
    The rule that tax follows the real substance of a transaction rather than its name or form. A payment labeled a dividend can be taxed as a kickback if that is what it is, but the tax authority must prove the substance.Appears in: EP.06
    Paid capital reduction
    Reducing capital by paying shareholders to buy back and cancel shares. When a holding company does it, cash flows back to its owners.Appears in: EP.03
    Acquisition financing
    Loans taken to fund buying a company. Private equity commonly stacks such loans on top to buy companies (a leveraged buyout).Appears in: EP.03
    Captive market
    A market built on customers effectively guaranteed within a buyer or a group. Critics call it funneling business to affiliates.Appears in: EP.02
    Related party
    Under the amended Medical Devices Act: relatives of a hospital founder within the second degree, holders of more than 50% of shares, or anyone who controls management regardless of shareholding.Appears in: EP.01 · EP.02 · EP.06
    'Petite' cosmetic procedures
    A press term for light, non-surgical cosmetic procedures such as Botox, fillers and lifting.Appears in: EP.05
